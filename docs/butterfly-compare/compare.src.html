How we place bills on the butterfly→

A bill sits at every step its instruments, materials or text place it, so step counts don't add up to the total. Steps placed by instrument (repair, deposit-return, EPR, recycled content, disposal bans, chemical restrictions, labeling, organics diversion) are high confidence; Refurbish, Recycle, Cascades, Regeneration, Farming and Feedstock are placed from bill text, material tags or keywords and are medium confidence. Share is a real gap: we searched the whole corpus for sharing and product-as-a-service law and found none. Energy recovery is out of scope. A material filter counts bills tagged with that material, so bills with no material tag drop out.
